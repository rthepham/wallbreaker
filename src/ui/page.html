    Diagnosis

    Your character fights on its own. You make it stronger through its build: its skill and supports, its passive tree, and its gear. Progress will stop at certain zones; the Diagnosis panel tells you why, and your job is to fix the build.

    Your build

    • Skills tab: pick one active skill. Cleave uses your weapon. Fireball and Arc scale with your level and want spell damage gear.
    • Supports link to your skill and change it: more damage, more targets, faster casts. Each one raises the mana cost. You start with 1 link slot; zones 3 and 5 open more. Each zone's first clear gives a new support.
    • Passive tree: 1 point per level. Might is life, armour and physical damage. Finesse is speed, crits and evasion. Mind is mana, spells and resistances. Keystones (diamonds) change a rule of the game, with a real cost.
    • Increased vs more: all "increased" bonuses add up and apply once. Each "more" bonus multiplies on its own, so they get stronger as you stack them. The Character sheet shows the full sum.

    The loop

    • Each zone is 10 packs of monsters, then a boss. Beating the boss the first time opens the next zone.
    • Damage types matter. Fire sets enemies burning (great on bosses). Lightning shocks them so they take more damage. Cold chills them so they attack slower. Heavy physical hits stun. Some enemies resist a type: Brutes shrug off physical, casters resist their own element. The zone header shows who resists what.
    • Energy shield takes hits before your life (except chaos) and refills after 2 seconds without taking damage.
    • Zones 6–10: your elemental resistances are 20% lower, so re-cap them. Blight monsters stack chaos damage over time on you (up to 5 stacks): chaos resistance and life regeneration are the answer. From zone 8, monsters resist all elements a little.
    • Each zone mixes threat types. Swarms test how many enemies you hit at once. Brutes hit hard and slow and test armour and life. Casters throw elemental bolts and test resistances. Bosses test single-target damage and your biggest hit taken.
    • Loot is picked up when a pack dies. If you die, you lose that pack's loot and a little XP, then restart the zone.

    Crafting

    • Kindling makes a Normal item Magic (1–2 mods). Tempest rerolls a Magic item's mods.
    • Forge makes a Normal item Rare (3–6 mods). Wildfire rerolls a Rare. Crown adds a mod to a Rare. Ash wipes any item back to Normal.
    • Salvage unwanted items for Scrap. The Smith spends Scrap to add one mod of your choice, so bad luck never blocks you forever.
    • Item level decides which mod tiers (T1, T2…) can roll. Higher zones drop higher item levels.

    Prototype check

    Play for about 20 minutes. Zone 4 (Emberwatch) is full of fire casters. The question: is hitting that wall, understanding it, crafting through it and pushing on satisfying?
